### Full description

```text
Wind Brass Tuner is a focused pitch tuner for wind and brass players.

Choose your instrument transposition, select a written target note, set the A4 reference, and play a steady tone. The app converts the written pitch to concert pitch and shows the detected frequency, cents, sharp or flat guidance, and a clear tuning meter.

Features:
• Real-time microphone pitch detection
• Concert, B-flat, E-flat, and F instrument modes
• Target notes A4, B-flat4, C5, D5, E-flat5, F5, and G5
• A4 reference from 440 Hz to 444 Hz
• Stable-tone indication
• On-device audio processing

Privacy-friendly by design:
• No account required
• No ads
• No analytics or tracking
• No audio recording
• No audio upload

Wind Brass Tuner is designed for individual practice, rehearsal preparation, lessons, long-tone work, and quick concert-pitch checks.
```

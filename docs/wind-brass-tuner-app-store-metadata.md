## Description Draft

```text
Wind and Brass Tuner is a focused pitch tuner for wind and brass players.

It is designed for musicians who need quick concert-pitch checking with common transpositions:

- Concert Pitch
- B-flat Instrument
- E-flat Instrument
- F Instrument

Select your instrument, choose the target note, set your A4 reference, and play a steady tone. The app shows detected frequency, cents, sharp/flat guidance, and a clear meter for tuning.

Version 1 includes:

- Real-time microphone pitch detection
- Concert pitch conversion for B-flat, E-flat, and F instruments
- Target notes A4, B-flat4, C5, D5, E-flat5, F5, and G5
- A4 reference options from 440 Hz to 444 Hz
- Stable tone indication
- On-device audio processing

Privacy-friendly by design:

- No account required
- No ads
- No third-party analytics
- No audio recording
- No audio upload

Wind and Brass Tuner is intended for practice, rehearsal preparation, lesson support, and quick pitch checks.
```

## App Review Notes Draft

```text
Wind and Brass Tuner uses the microphone only to analyze instrument pitch in real time. Audio is processed on device and is not recorded, saved, or uploaded. The app does not require login, does not include ads, and does not use third-party analytics.

To test:
1. Launch the app.
2. Tap Start and allow microphone access.
3. Play or generate a steady tone near the selected target note.
4. Confirm that Hz, cents, sharp/flat guidance, and the tuning meter respond.
```
